# सेतु · SETU

**Setu** means *bridge*. This one connects a question about an Indian MSME scheme — asked in English, Hindi, Tamil, or a mix — to the official page that actually answers it.

Every claim here traces back to a cited government source. Eligibility is never guessed by a language model — it's decided by deterministic rules that read your situation against a scheme's published criteria and nothing else.

**Try asking, in any language:**

- *What documents are required for ZED certification?*
- *ZED प्रमाणन के लिए कौन से दस्तावेज़ आवश्यक हैं?*
- *MSME skill training scheme mein trainee ki age limit kya hai?*
- *இந்த திட்டத்திற்கு யார் தகுதியானவர்?*

If a scheme isn't in the current sources, SETU says so — rather than inventing an answer.
